The GLBs are embedded as Unity TextAssets. Each carries its mesh, skin weights, textures, and Stand, Walk, Attack and Death clips. The Windows prototype also supports loading these files from StreamingAssets/Frontend/BlackHand. Unit roles and team variants are assigned by BlackHandArt.

## Black Hand interface art

The emblem and the 26 ability and status icons were generated with built-in ImageGen. The emblem prompt uses the existing Human, Centaur and Pepe round medallions as composition references for a blackened-steel knight with a raised hand on the shield. The three skill-sheet prompts use Pepe ability art as the visual reference for painterly square icons with iron weapons, bronze frames, vivid sparks and individually colored status effects. The 15 portraits in the production card and group selection are renders of the fighter models. The VS portrait uses built-in ImageGen. Prompt theme: an original blackened-steel medieval veteran with an oxblood cloak and a kite shield bearing a hand emblem, set before slate castle ruins at dusk.
